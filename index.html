Learn WorkBuddy with Phoebe

Two tracks into the agent that finishes your office work

Tencent's WorkBuddy takes one plain-language instruction, plans the steps, executes them on your computer, and hands back a finished file - "you say, it does." A leader track teaches you to judge where it pays off, govern the risk, and roll it out to a team without touching the tool. An operator track builds one real deliverable per session - the market-entry pack for Meridian, a Singapore tea brand entering Malaysia - through data analysis, research, decks, connectors, and scheduled automation. An instruction-quality simulator runs live in your browser on the operator pages: five levers, ten tasks, a scorecard that climbs as your instructions sharpen.
